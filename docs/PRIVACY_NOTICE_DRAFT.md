## Who we are
[Hooze Enterprises, address, contact email/phone, data-protection contact].
The business you are chatting with ("the Business") decides why your messages are used. We run the assistant for it.

## What we collect when you message the Business on WhatsApp
- Your WhatsApp phone number and the name on your WhatsApp profile.
- What you write or say (voice notes are turned into text), and the assistant's and the Business's replies.
- Details you give so the Business can help you: for example what you want to order or buy, delivery address and area,
  preferred times for a visit, budget.
- For orders: what you ordered, the amount, and payment status/reference. We do not see or store your card details;
  payment is handled by Paystack.

## Why we use it
To answer you, take your order or booking, take payment, deliver, send the follow-up messages described below, keep
records of sales, and keep the service secure and working.

## Follow-up messages

are kept without your personal details, because the Business needs them for its accounts.
